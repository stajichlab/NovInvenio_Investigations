serinales vs pichiales: functional enrichment (novelty)

One-sided hypergeometric test per term; Benjamini-Hochberg FDR within each scope, level and vocabulary. Candidates are the novelty candidates: gene families present in the IN group and absent from the OUT group. Universe: proteome. Background clustered: True (102522 sequences in 23051 clusters, mmseqs easy-cluster --min-seq-id 0.3 -c 0.8). Family level: one unit per candidate family and per background cluster.

Sources: Pfam: 2026-01-27-Pfam38.2; InterPro entries: 110.0; GO: releases/2026-07-26; analysis generated 2026-10-12T15:21:05. Source tables: studies/fungi/serinales_vs_pichiales/analysis/enrichment/outputs/proteome_clustered. Full record: PROVENANCE. Run report: report.html.

Caveats
  • Annotation provenance is not fully recorded: PROVENANCE.yaml lists the per-species annotation files and their checksums, but not the UniProt release they come from.
  • GO results are secondary. GO annotations include computational evidence (IEA, IBA); the GO evidence column shows if a term has non-IEA support.
  • Candidate families (pipeline clustering of candidates) and background clusters (mmseqs clustering of the background proteome) are made by different runs, so family-level counts are not fully comparable.
  • Low annotation: the candidate set has less than 50% annotation for Pfam 40.8%, InterPro 47.4%, GO 28.5%, any vocabulary 48.5%. Enrichment for these vocabularies has low power.
