# PF11001 (AFUB_07903_YDR124W_hel) in pangenome islands: check (2026-10-17)

Question: PF11001 is island-enriched in A. fumigatus and in Coccidioides. Is this a real shared signal or a coincidence?

Analysis directory: `studies/fungi/Afumigatus_pangenome/analysis/pf11001/` (scripts, HMM, hmmsearch output, A. fumigatus tables, figures). Coccidioides tables: `studies/fungi/coccidioides_pangenome/analysis/pf11001/tables/`. Nothing was committed.

## 1. The domain

- Pfam 38.2 (`Pfam-A.hmm.dat`): ID AFUB_07903_YDR124W_hel, AC PF11001.15, DE "YDR124W-like, helical bundle domain", type Domain, GA 31.3, model length 146. The HMM header gives NSEQ 80. Pfam-C has no clan entry for PF11001.
- UniProt names all 5 Af293 proteins with this domain "Subtelomeric hrmA-associated cluster protein" (`annotations/UP000002530.tsv.gz`). The Pfam ID refers to the A1163 gene AFUB_079030 and the S. cerevisiae gene YDR124W.

## 2. How the pipeline test counts (pinned clone a2836adb)

- `bin/pangenome_select_background_reps.py`: background = families with `bin` shell or cloud in `frequency_table.tsv`. Core, soft_core, singleton, nonrep_only and outgroup_only families are excluded.
- `modules/pangenome/pfam_enrichment.nf`: `hmmscan --domE 1e-3` (param `pangenome_pfam_domain_evalue`) of the background representatives only. It does not use `--cut_ga`.
- `bin/pangenome_domain_enrichment.py`: a family has a domain when one domain row has i-Evalue <= 1e-3. Island set = union of `member_families` of all rows of `significant_islands.tsv`, intersected with the background. `n_with_domain_in_islands` = background families with the domain that are in the island set. `n_with_domain_in_background` = all background families with the domain. Test = one-sided Fisher (greater). BH-FDR is applied over the domains that occur in at least one island family.

## 6. Coincidence checks

- **(a) Taxonomic spread.** Survey of 131 species names (`survey_by_species_dedup.tsv`). There are 0 hits in 11 non-fungal species and 0 hits in 30 non-Dikarya fungal species (Mucoromycota, Zoopagomycota, Chytridiomycota, Blastocladiomycota, Neocallimastigomycota). 68 of the remaining 90 Dikarya species have hits: Saccharomycotina, Pezizomycotina, Taphrinomycotina (Neolecta), and Basidiomycota (Ustilaginomycotina, Malassezia, Tremellomycetes, Pucciniomycotina). Outside Pezizomycotina, most species have 1 copy. Species without a hit include S. pombe, Agaricales (3), Tilletiales, all 9 Metschnikowiaceae/Candidozyma species, and C. glabrata and K. lactis. So the domain is not restricted to Pezizomycotina in these proteomes.
  - metschnikowiaceae_cah loss test (whole_set, family, Pfam): PF11001 has the smallest p of 1,160 Pfam terms tested. 11 of 73 annotated candidate families carry it, and 11 of 11 background families with the domain are candidates (fold 190, p 3.8e-26, q 4.4e-23). Path: `studies/fungi/metschnikowiaceae_cah/analysis/enrichment/outputs/loss/proteome_clustered/enrichment_domains.tsv.gz` line 2264. The lost families are 1-copy genes in 12 of 13 outgroup Serinales/Pichiales/Alaninales species. This is a loss of a conserved single-copy gene, not an island signal.
- **(b) Paralog adjacency vs family count.** Tandem copies are rare: 0 in Coccidioides, 19 genes in clusters of 2-3 in Af. In Af, the 21 counted island families form 18 island-disjoint groups. In Coccidioides, the families group more: v5 8 → 5 groups, v2 14 → 8 groups. The A. fumigatus PF11001 genes have nearby HrmA, APH, DUF3723 and DUF3435 genes more often than the genome background (`neighbourhood_marks.tsv`, +/-5 genes, family-rep Pfam):

  | neighbour within 5 genes | PF11001 genes | genome background |
  |---|---|---|
  | HrmA | 104/1,133 (9.2%) | 0.06% |
  | APH | 5.0% | 0.9% |
  | DUF3723 | 5.1% | 0.2% |
  | DUF3435 | 7.9% | 0.4% |

  The Fisher p values use genes across strains as units, so they are not independent tests. In Coccidioides (v5 positions) there are 0 HrmA and 0 DUF3723 neighbours, APH is 3.6% vs 4.4% background, and DUF3435 is 1.8% vs 0.9%.
- **(c) Independence and combined p.** The A. fumigatus and Coccidioides pangenomes are separate, so the Af p can be combined with one Coccidioides run. v5 and v2 share genomes and are not independent. Fisher's method:
  - Af × v5: 1.5e-5.
  - Af × v2: 1.3e-6.
  - All three: 5.8e-8. This value is not valid because v5 and v2 share genomes.

  These values do not correct for PF11001 having been picked after looking at the tables. There were 1,977 + 928 / 1,325 domains tested; 549 (v5) and 715 (v2) domains were tested in both runs. By combined p, PF11001 ranks 13th in both pairs.

  Shared island enrichment is common:
  - p <= 0.05 in both runs: 19 domains (Af × v5) and 25 (Af × v2). If the runs were independent, 4.9 and 7.2 would be expected.
  - p <= PF11001's p in both runs: 5 domains in both pairs (PF11001, APH, DUF3435, DUF3723, DUF3723_N).
  - q < 0.05 in both runs: 4 domains for Af × v5 (PF11001 is not among them) and the same 5 domains for Af × v2.
- **(d) Neighbouring domains.** See section 3. In each run several domains with similar small counts have similar p, for example Patatin 14/14 in v2 and Exo_endo_phos_2 13/13 in Af. With 8-24 families per domain, one family more or less moves p by about one order of magnitude.
- **(e) Starship / captain** (`starship_captain_overlap.tsv`):
  - Af293 Starship element families: 2 PF11001 families vs 0.20 expected. p 0.017 when the universe is all 47,954 families; p 0.20 for shell+cloud only (1 vs 0.23).
  - DUF3435 captain families: 0 PF11001 families in every run.
  - The two Starship-overlapping genes are AFUA_1G00780 (Lamia h4) and AFUA_5G14920 (Nebuchadnezzar h1).

## 8. Not verified

- The definition and quality of the islands themselves (co-occurrence and physical calls) were not re-examined. Island instances in Af293 come from `starship_island_overlap/full_v072/per_island.tsv.gz`.
- Neighbour domains are family-representative labels (`--cut_ga`), not per-gene scans.
- No phylogeny of the PF11001 proteins was built, so it is unknown whether the accessory A. fumigatus and Coccidioides copies are one lineage.
- Strains are not independent units for the per-gene rates.
- The survey uses only local proteomes (131 species). No online Pfam/InterPro taxonomy was checked.
- The UniProt "hrmA-associated cluster" annotation was not checked against the primary literature.
- Coccidioides contig ends are not telomeres.
- Starship elements are only annotated for Af293.
