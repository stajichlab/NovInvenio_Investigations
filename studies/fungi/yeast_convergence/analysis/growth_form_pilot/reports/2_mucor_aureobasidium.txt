SPECIES: Mucor circinelloides (strains named M. lusitanicus not found in sources read)
CODING_SUPPORTED_BY_EVIDENCE: dimorphic
DOMINANT_PHASE (if dimorphic): hyphal
OVERALL_NOTE: Several independent papers describe or test dimorphism. The aerobic standard-culture form is hyphal. Yeast forms need anaerobic/high CO2 conditions, calcineurin inhibition, or elevated temperature. I did not search the "M. lusitanicus" name, so no source using it was checked.
